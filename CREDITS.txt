EchLub DAW browser demo: credits and licences

EchLub is a digital audio workstation. This page runs its engine and app, compiled
to WebAssembly, in your browser. Only the compiled result is published; the source is not.
中文：這是 EchLub DAW 在瀏覽器裡的 demo。只公開編譯後的結果，原始碼不公開。

Third-party software

  libraries (egui, eframe, wgpu, winit, wasm-bindgen and others). Their names, versions,
  licences and licence texts are in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt.
